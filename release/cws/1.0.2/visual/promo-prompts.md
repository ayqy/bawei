# bawei CWS small promo prompts

三套候选固定使用本仓库 `public/icons/icon-128.png` 作为品牌角色参考，只改变视觉系统。每套原始图均需通过 built-in `image_gen` 单独生成，保留原图后再归一化到 440×280。

## v1 clarity-first

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Chrome Web Store 440×280 small promotional image source art
Input image: the local `public/icons/icon-128.png` is the brand-character reference; preserve its friendly round pink octopus silhouette, dark navy outline, simple face, and soft blue outer background family.
Scene/backdrop: clean deep navy-to-slate flat backdrop with subtle paper texture, no gradient banding.
Subject: one large friendly pink octopus on the left gently holding a single white article card; from that card, a clean curved line branches to several small unbranded rounded destination dots on the right.
Style/medium: polished flat editorial vector-like illustration with crisp shapes and restrained depth.
Composition/framing: exact 11:7 landscape intent, strong single focal point, generous padding, readable at thumbnail size; keep nodes sparse and orderly.
Color palette: pink, deep navy, muted blue, white, one restrained cyan accent.
Text: none.
Constraints: communicate one article distributed to multiple destinations; no platform logos, no real or invented UI, no checkmarks, no success counts, no browser chrome, no watermark.
Avoid: tiny details, busy network diagrams, extra characters, photorealism, neon glow, illegible symbols.
